About the pro rules, sources & patch notes

Patch currency — verified 20 Nov 2026: Minor Update 11-04-2026 (Valve, posted 5 Nov 2026) is still the live balance patch, confirmed across Steam news, SteamDB, the Deadlock Handbook and Deadlock Builder; later builds (6860/6861) are silent with no confirmed balance changes. Roster verified at 44 heroes — Mina is a hero; Nurse Harrow is the newest; no 45th hero announced.

Where the pro rules come from: the Deadlock Handbook counter-building cheat sheet (match data since 5 Nov 2026); Deadlock Item Builder's per-hero counter pages (17 read in full, stats refreshed 11–20 Nov 2026); Guides-Factory's counter-items guide; DenyPick; Valve's 11-04-2026 patch notes; and two older per-hero lists kept only where corroborated and capped at Thin confidence — Esports Insider (Jul 2025) and Poketape (Oct 2024), with item names translated to current ones. deadlock.coach was checked and carries no counter-buy content, so nothing here cites it. “Pro” here means high-MMR / guide-author consensus — no source named an individual pro player behind a rule (the closest named creator is Ansonex's counter-items video, via Guides-Factory).

Confidence: Strong = 2+ independent current sources agree. Moderate = 1 current source plus principle corroboration, or 2 older sources. Thin = a single older source — Infernus, Viscous and Yamato lean on these most; verify in game.

Caveats carried from the validation sweep: no reveal/true-sight counter-buy item exists in any source searched — stealth has no item answer here, and none was invented. Toxic Bullets' healing cut is disputed between current sources (−35% vs −40%) and Decay's too (−50% vs −55%) — check the live shop tooltip before quoting a number. Healbane stacking is settled by Valve: 2 stacks ≈54.7%, 4 stacks ≈72% after 4 Nov. Anti-heal does not shrink barriers (Plot Armor, Royal Pestments) and does not prevent Victor's revive. Dispel Magic does NOT remove Pocket's Affliction — Divine Barrier is the only cleanse that does. A few item categories (Tankbuster, Escalating Exposure, Focus Lens) weren't re-confirmed in current sources; their costs and tiers were.

The item pickers list the complete shop — all 156 items: Weapon (53), Vitality (54) and Spirit (49) — cross-checked item-by-item against the Deadlock Wiki item list.
