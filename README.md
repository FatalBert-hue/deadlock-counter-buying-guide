# Deadlock Counter Buying Guide — v0.8

> Pick the enemy hero and their items, pick your hero and yours, and get the counter-buy the pros would buy.

**Live guide:** https://fatalbert-hue.github.io/deadlock-counter-buying-guide/

## What's inside

- **44 heroes** — every hero in the current roster, with portraits
- **156 items** — the full in-game shop (Weapon, Vitality, Spirit), with icons, tier, and souls cost
- **Full build mode** — 12 item slots per side, just like an in-game build
- **Quick pick mode** — just the enemy hero and the item type they're buying (Gun, Health, or Spirit) for a fast answer
- **Pro counter-buy rules** — suggestions based on what high-level players actually buy, with confidence and sources for each pick

## How to use it

1. Pick the **enemy hero** and the items in their build.
2. Pick **your hero** and the items in your build.
3. Hit **See counter buy** and get a top pick with reasons, plus alternates.
